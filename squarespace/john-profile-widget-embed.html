John McArthur

General Manager, Markham Office

With over 30 years of experience in leveraging technology to meet client needs, John McArthur brings a strong background in Electronics Engineering Technology and a career rooted in innovation and client success.

He began his professional journey at a Canadian startup, introducing RF technology solutions to the grocery industry. His early roles encompassed RF design, operational engineering, project management, and sales engineering.

Throughout his career, he has worked directly with industry giants such as Kroger, Loblaws, Stop & Shop, A&P, and Safeway UK, helping to deliver impactful solutions.

He later joined a Canadian market-making company focused on developing cutting-edge technology solutions, where he held key roles including Product Manager and Director of North American Sales.

His experience expanded globally through collaborations with major international clients such as MGM Resorts International, Sands Corporation, and The Wynn Group.

For the past 13 years, he has been a vital part of Pineapple Bytes, focusing on selecting the right products and services to serve client needs.
