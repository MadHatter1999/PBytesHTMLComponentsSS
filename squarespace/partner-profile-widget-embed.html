Frank Naccarato - Partner

Frank Naccarato is a seasoned technology executive and partner at Pineapple Bytes, bringing over 40 years of experience in driving innovation and growth across the restaurant, retail, and financial technology sectors. His expertise lies in developing and delivering exceptional technology solutions tailored to client needs.

Before joining Pineapple Bytes, Frank founded Radeon Technologies Ltd., a leading provider of reliable POS systems and support services for restaurant and retail businesses. Under his leadership, Radeon Technologies earned a strong reputation for exceptional service, resulting in long-term client relationships and sustained business growth. The company recently merged with Pineapple Bytes, where Frank now serves as a partner.

Throughout his career, Frank has built strategic partnerships with industry leaders, enhancing product offerings and expanding market reach. As a System Partner with NCR Aloha, he has delivered valuable warranty and support options to hospitality POS clients.

Frank's previous experience includes holding technology and territory management roles at a multinational corporation. He also owned and operated a technology dealership, transforming it into a national player through strategic sales initiatives and business development.

With his extensive experience and proven track record, Frank is a valuable asset to Pineapple Bytes, where he remains committed to driving innovation and delivering outstanding technology solutions.
