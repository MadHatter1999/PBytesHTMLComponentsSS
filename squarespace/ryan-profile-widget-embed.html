Ryan Parnell

General Manager, Dartmouth Office

As General Manager of our Dartmouth, Nova Scotia office, Ryan leads the branch with a focus on operational excellence, team development, and customer satisfaction. He plays a key role in aligning local execution with company-wide strategy, ensuring our clients in the retail and hospitality sectors continue to receive exceptional service and support.

Ryan began his career in the hospitality industry in 2003 as the first member of a technical team supporting point-of-sale systems. Over the past 20 years, he has held several key positions including Inside Sales, Service Manager, Onsite Installer, Procurement Officer, and Senior Operations Manager. This diverse experience has equipped him with a deep understanding of both client needs and technical operations.

Known for his attention to detail and commitment to delivering value, Ryan has built lasting relationships with clients and team members alike. His leadership and expertise continue to be a driving force behind the success of Pineapple Bytes.
